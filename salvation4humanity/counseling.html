Getting Started

It's simple. No hoops, no waiting lists, no cost.

1
Reach Out

Send a message through our community channels or use the contact form below. Say as much or as little as you want.

2

Pastor Clay will reach out personally to schedule a conversation — voice, video, or text. Whatever you're comfortable with.

3
Walk Forward Together

This isn't a one-time thing. We walk with you as long as you need. Ongoing support, ongoing hope.
