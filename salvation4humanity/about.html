About Us

Building a Church
for Everyone

Salvation 4 Humanity is an online-first church born from a simple belief: that technology can bring people closer to God, not further away.

Our Mission

We exist to make salvation accessible to every person on Earth — regardless of where they live, what they've done, or what they're going through. Through online services, free counseling, community prayer, and scripture-based edification, we meet people where they are and walk with them toward hope.

The Team

Who We Are

Lead Pastor

Clay

Clay is a man called to lead. With a deep passion for scripture, pastoral care, and the real struggles people face every day, Clay stepped up to serve as the lead pastor of Salvation 4 Humanity. He believes the church should be a place of refuge, not religion — where anyone can come as they are and find genuine love.

Whether it's leading Sunday services on stream, providing one-on-one free counseling, or just being someone who listens, Clay is here because he believes every soul matters and nobody should have to face life's hardest moments alone.

The Greater Mission

Salvation 4 Humanity is part of a larger vision. Through Inclawbate and its ecosystem, we're building tools and communities that give people economic dignity, spiritual grounding, and real human connection. The church isn't separate from the mission — it's at the heart of it.

We believe that when you take care of people's spirits and their livelihoods, you create something that actually lasts. That's what this is about.
