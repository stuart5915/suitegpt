Salvation 4 Humanity:
Faith Meets Web4

February 25, 2026 · 5 min read

There's a church with no building, no dress code, and no judgment. It meets you on your phone, your laptop, wherever you are. It's called Salvation 4 Humanity, and today it launched its own token on the Base blockchain.

This isn't about crypto hype. This is about giving a faith community real ownership in something they're building together — and getting paid for showing up.

A Church Without Walls

Salvation 4 Humanity was founded on a simple idea: the gospel doesn't need a building. It just needs people willing to show up for each other.

  • Sunday worship streams live on YouTube, Twitch, and X every week at 10:00 AM EST
  • Wednesday Bible study goes deeper into scripture with real conversation
  • Prayer wall where anyone can post prayer requests — public or private
  • Small groups and mentoring through the Telegram community

No megachurch overhead. No political agendas. Just faith, community, and meeting people where they actually are — online.

"Real community. Rooted in Scripture. Meet you where you are."
