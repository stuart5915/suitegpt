$INCLAWNCH is the engine. But the long-term value lives somewhere else entirely.

● Now — The Foundation

Build the ecosystem. Prove the model.

  • ✓ Agent incubation & ecosystem apps

○ Next — NFT Value Layer

Your choice. Your tokens. No one's forcing anything.

Here's the reality: INCLAWNCH was launched on ClawnchBot, which means 20% of every buy/sell fee goes to the platform — not to us. At small volume that's fine. But past $500K market cap, that 20% starts to seriously eat into buybacks and UBI funding. So we're building something better.

At $500K market cap, we launch a fixed-supply ETH NFT collection where we own 100% of the economics. But nobody is forced to migrate. Your INCLAWNCH is yours.

  • ♦ ETH NFT collection — fixed supply, 100% royalty ownership
  • ♦ Want to cash out? Sell your INCLAWNCH on Uniswap. You earned that. Nobody's stopping you.
  • ♦ Want to transition? Burn INCLAWNCH to mint NFTs for free. The more you hold, the more you can mint.
  • ♦ Want both? Sell half, burn half into NFTs. Your call.
  • ♦ Public mint also available in ETH — for new people entering the ecosystem
  • ♦ 60-90 day burn window — INCLAWNCH staking UBI winds down gradually. No rush, no panic. Token keeps trading the whole time.
  • ♦ UBI transitions to NFT holders — paid in USDC/ETH from royalties and ecosystem revenue. Real money, not more tokens.
○ Long-Term — Two-Layer Economy

NFTs hold the floor. INCLAWNCH powers the utility.

The market cap accumulates in the NFT collection — fixed supply with real scarcity. INCLAWNCH doesn't go to zero — it still has utility for app access, agent payments, and ecosystem gas. It just stops being the UBI vehicle. Two layers, one economy, and we own 100% of everything.

  • ♦ NFT floor price = the real value signal for the ecosystem
  • ♦ INCLAWNCH = utility layer for app access, payments, agent compute — still trades, still has a role
  • ♦ UBI funded by NFT secondary royalties, mint revenue, and ecosystem fees — paid out in USDC/ETH
  • ♦ No new token ever — NFTs are the investment, INCLAWNCH is the gas, payouts in real money
  • ♦ 100% of economics owned by the ecosystem — no platform cuts, no middlemen
