Marketing Guide

How to promote your project

The Playbook

Step 1
Write your pitch

One sentence that explains what your project is and why someone should care. Lead with the benefit.

Step 2
Create visuals

Logo, banner, memes. People scroll fast — eye-catching visuals stop the thumb. Use AI tools to generate them.

Step 3
Build a community

Create a Telegram or Discord. Even 10 engaged people beats 1,000 lurkers. Make it a place people want to hang out.

Step 4
Post schedule

Launch day: announce everywhere. Week 1: daily updates. Ongoing: 2-3 posts per day, mix content types.

Tips

  • Post consistently — 2-3 times daily minimum. Algorithms reward frequency.
  • Engage, don't shill. Reply to others, join conversations, add value before you promote.
  • Build in public. Share progress, milestones, numbers. Transparency builds trust.
  • Use your AI agent. Launch one from the Tools page to auto-post on X and keep momentum.
  • Reward your community. Airdrops, shoutouts, roles — make holders feel like owners.
