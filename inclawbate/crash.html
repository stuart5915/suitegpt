How to Play

Bet CLAWS, watch the multiplier climb, cash out before it crashes!

1
Deposit CLAWS Connect your wallet and deposit CLAWS tokens to get started. 1 CLAWS = 1 CLAWS in-game.
2
Place Your Bet During the countdown between rounds, enter your bet amount and hit "Place Bet".
3
Watch It Fly The multiplier starts at 1.00x and climbs. The higher it goes, the more you win!
4
Cash Out in Time Hit "Cash Out" before the round crashes. Your bet is multiplied by whatever you cashed out at.
5
Don't Get Greedy If you don't cash out before it crashes, you lose your entire bet for that round.
