Frequently Asked Questions

Is this really free?
Yes. We don't charge for development, hosting, or marketing. We earn when your app earns — through a revenue share model. If your app doesn't make money, you owe nothing.
Do I need technical skills?
No. You provide the idea and the leadership. Our team handles all the technical work — coding, design, deployment, and maintenance.
What kind of apps can you build?
Anything web-based: business tools, community platforms, marketplaces, dashboards, content sites, games, social apps. If it runs in a browser, we can build it.
How long does it take?
Most projects go from idea to live app within days to a couple of weeks, depending on complexity.
Do I own what's built?
Yes. You lead the project, you own the brand, you make the decisions. We're your team, not your boss.
