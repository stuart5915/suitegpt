How It Works

Three steps to better posture

Step 01
📷

Scan

Hit Start and your webcam activates. AI maps 33 body landmarks in real-time, drawing your skeleton on screen.

Step 02
🧠

Analyze

Forward head angle, shoulder imbalance, hip alignment, and spine curvature are measured every frame. Color-coded feedback shows what needs work.

Step 03
⚡

Fix

Take a snapshot to freeze your report card. See exactly what to correct. Track your posture score over time.

Features

Built for your body

💪

Real-Time Skeleton Overlay

33-point body landmark detection drawn live on your webcam feed. See exactly how AI reads your body alignment.

🏃

Color-Coded Joints

Green means good. Yellow means watch it. Red means fix it. Every joint is color-coded so you instantly know where the problem is.

📋

Instant Report Card

Snapshot your scan and get a detailed breakdown — forward head angle, shoulder tilt, hip level, spine curvature, and overall score.

🔒

100% Private

No data leaves your device. No uploads. No servers. MediaPipe AI runs entirely in your browser. Your body, your data.
