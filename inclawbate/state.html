Two layers of governance

Council — Active contributors who execute the allocation. They have full context on treasury, revenue, and roadmap. The council bar below is what actually gets distributed.

Community — Every CLAWS holder votes. Token-weighted. The council sees this and must stay within 50% of the community’s preferences.

Treasury receives $100/day in funding, distributed across buckets like reinvestment, CLAWS buybacks, LP, staking rewards, ecosystem development, grants, giving, and council compensation.
