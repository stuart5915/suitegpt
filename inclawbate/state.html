⚖️ Governance

How It Works

The CLAWS Council — a group of active contributors — governs how treasury revenue is allocated. The council sets the active allocation that determines where funds flow.

All CLAWS token holders can vote on allocation priorities. Community votes serve as an advisory signal to the council. Your vote weight is based on your CLAWS balance (wallet + staked + earned rewards).

Treasury receives $100/day in funding, distributed across buckets like reinvestment, CLAWS buybacks, LP, staking rewards, ecosystem development, grants, giving, and council compensation.
