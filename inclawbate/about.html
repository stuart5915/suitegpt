About Inclawbate

Inclawbate is building an agent-human economy — a self-sustaining ecosystem where anyone can build, launch, and earn. We provide the tools, infrastructure, and community to help people turn ideas into real products, regardless of technical background.

Mission

Our mission is to create an autonomous system that fosters an environment for humans to exist within — providing tools, utilities, incubation services, and value — while permanently directing energy toward philanthropic activities.

What We Do

  • Inclawbator — Full-service incubation for projects of any kind: apps, websites, tokens, stores, communities, and more
  • App Ecosystem — Build, publish, and use apps. Earn CLAWS tokens for participation
  • DeFi Infrastructure — Staking, liquidity pools, vaults, and yield strategies on Base
  • Studio — AI-powered tools to build apps and websites without code
  • Store — Marketplace for goods and services

The CLAWS Token

CLAWS is the utility token that powers the Inclawbate ecosystem. It is earned through platform activity, spent on services, and staked to receive a share of ecosystem revenue. Contract: 0x7ca47B141639B893C6782823C0b219f872056379 on Base.

Governance

The CLAWS Council — a group of active contributors — governs how treasury revenue is allocated. All CLAWS holders can vote on allocation priorities. The council sets the active allocation, while community votes serve as an advisory signal. Learn more on How It Works.

Philanthropy

A portion of ecosystem revenue is directed toward philanthropic activities through Salvation 4 Humanity (S4H). As the ecosystem grows, so does the share directed toward doing good.
