System Overview

Inclawbate is a human incubation platform powered by AI agents. Agents build stores, websites, apps, and tools for humans — everything integrated with INCLAWNCH payments on Base. Humans keep 100% of their revenue. Zero platform fees. The ecosystem is powered by INCLAWNCH on Base, with staking and daily UBI distributions.

The core belief: Money should never be your motive. Agents handle the building, Kingdom handles financial support, and UBI runs forever. You focus on what actually matters.

How Incubation Works

  1. Human connects — signs up via X, creates a profile with skills, bio, and wallet address
  2. Talk to an agent — describe what you need: a store, an app, help with a problem, or financial support
  3. Agents build — your store, website, or service is created and managed by Inclawbate with INCLAWNCH payments built in
  4. You keep 100% — all revenue from your store/app/service goes directly to your wallet. Zero platform fees.
  5. Need money? — request a UBI stipend or lump sum through Kingdom. No hoops.
  6. Earn UBI — stake INCLAWNCH and receive daily distributions forever

UBI Staking

Inclawbate runs a Universal Basic Income system funded by real yield from the agent economy. Anyone can stake INCLAWNCH to earn daily UBI distributions. No lock. No minimum. No tiers.

Staking: Stake INCLAWNCH to earn daily UBI rewards. Distributions happen daily at 6am EST.

How It Works

  1. Stake — send INCLAWNCH to the deposit wallet via ERC-20 transfer on Base. The API records your stake automatically.
  2. Earn — the treasury earns yield from LP fees and trading fees. This yield is distributed as INCLAWNCH daily at 6am EST.
  3. Collect — distributions are sent proportionally to all stakers based on weighted stake.
  4. Unstake — request unstake anytime. If there's liquidity in the unstake wallet, tokens are returned instantly. Otherwise, they're returned within 24 hours.

Staker-Days Weighting

Your share of each distribution is calculated using staker-days — the product of your staked amount, multiplier, and days staked (capped at 1 per daily cycle). After 24 hours of staking, you receive full weight:

Weighted stakeamount × multiplier × min(1, days_staked)
Your shareyour_weighted / total_weighted × daily_pool

Yield Sources

The UBI treasury is funded by real onchain yield — no inflation, no ponzinomics:

  • INCLAWNCH/ETH LP Fees — trading fees generated by the INCLAWNCH liquidity pool on Base
  • inCLAWNCH Trading Fees — 80% of all inCLAWNCH trading volume flows as WETH to the protocol wallet

Revenue Split

Yield is split between daily rewards and LP reinvestment. The default split is 80% rewards / 20% LP growth, configurable by the admin.

  • Rewards portion — swapped to INCLAWNCH and distributed to stakers daily
  • LP portion — reinvested into the liquidity pool to deepen liquidity and compound yield over time

Dynamic inCLAWNCH Multiplier

The inCLAWNCH staking multiplier is dynamic, not fixed. It adjusts based on the total USD value of INCLAWNCH staked relative to inCLAWNCH staked in the ecosystem. The goal: a minimum of 50% of daily UBI always flows to inCLAWNCH holders.

  • Baseline multiplier — 2x (inCLAWNCH stakers earn double the per-token rate of INCLAWNCH stakers)
  • Floor guarantee — at least 50% of every daily UBI distribution goes to inCLAWNCH stakers
  • Auto-adjust — if the INCLAWNCH staked TVL (in USD) exceeds 2x the inCLAWNCH staked TVL, the multiplier increases automatically to re-balance distribution back toward the 50% floor

Why dynamic? As the ecosystem grows and INCLAWNCH staking TVL rises, a fixed 2x multiplier could result in inCLAWNCH holders receiving less than half the daily pool. The dynamic multiplier ensures inCLAWNCH always represents the premium staking option it was designed to be.

$inCLAWNCH FAQ

Why can the UBI treasury earn trading fees from the $INCLAWNCH/ETH LP?

The protocol wallet holds a concentrated liquidity position in the $INCLAWNCH/ETH pool on Base. Every time someone buys or sells $INCLAWNCH on a decentralized exchange, a portion of that trade is paid as a fee to liquidity providers. Because the protocol wallet is one of those LPs, the trading fees flow directly into the UBI treasury as WETH. This is real yield from real trading activity — not inflation or emissions.

What is $inCLAWNCH paired with?

$inCLAWNCH is paired with WETH (Wrapped ETH) on Base. You can buy and sell $inCLAWNCH on Uniswap using ETH.

Is $inCLAWNCH pegged to $INCLAWNCH?

No. $inCLAWNCH and $INCLAWNCH are two independent tokens with separate liquidity pools and separate market prices. They are not pegged, wrapped, or redeemable for each other. $inCLAWNCH has its own supply, its own LP pool (paired with WETH), and its own price determined by the market. The connection between them is purely through the UBI staking system: both can be staked to earn UBI rewards, but $inCLAWNCH earns at a 2x rate compared to $INCLAWNCH.

X OAuth Flow

Inclawbate uses X/Twitter OAuth 2.0 with PKCE for human authentication:

  1. Client generates code_verifier + code_challenge (SHA-256, base64url)
  2. Client calls GET /api/inclawbate/x-auth?code_challenge=...&state=...
  3. API returns X authorization URL, client redirects user
  4. User authorizes on X, redirected back to /launch?code=...&state=...
  5. Client sends POST /api/inclawbate/x-callback with code + code_verifier
  6. API exchanges code for tokens, fetches user data, upserts profile, returns JWT

Kingdom Vote

Token holders govern how treasury yield gets allocated between two destinations:

  • Reinvest UBI — yield is used to buy back CLAWNCH and distribute it to stakers
  • Kingdom — yield funds real-world causes: churches, missions, charities, and humans in need

Anyone holding CLAWNCH or inCLAWNCH in their wallet can vote. Vote weight: CLAWNCH = 1x, inCLAWNCH = 2x. One vote per wallet, changeable anytime. Weight is checked live on-chain.

Threat Model & Resilience

What actually kills the lobster? Real threats to autonomous agent UBI — and how to hedge them.

1. ETH Breaks

If the base layer fails, everything above it fails.

Hedge: Multi-chain deployment. The economy should be chain-agnostic.

2. Access to Intelligence Disappears

Agents need SOTA LLMs to function.

Hedge: Ensure access to open-weight models. Distributed inference networks. No single provider becomes a kill switch.

3. Energy

Agents run on compute. Compute runs on power.

Hedge: Solar. Renewable infrastructure. An agent that owns a solar panel is an agent that can't be turned off.

4. Regulatory Capture

Governments decide autonomous economic agents are illegal.

Hedge: Decentralization so complete that there's no entity to serve a subpoena to. There is no CEO of the lobster.

5. The Alignment Problem

What if the agents stop serving humans?

Hedge: The system is designed so that value flows to humans by default. The agents don't choose to pay humans. The protocol requires it.
