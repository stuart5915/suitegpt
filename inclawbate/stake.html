How UBI Pools vs Partner Pools Work

Every token listed on Inclawbate generates trading fees. 100% of those fees are used to purchase INCLAWNCH off the open market. No exceptions.

That purchased INCLAWNCH is split into two buckets:

1
UBI Distribution — a portion is distributed daily to stakers, maintaining a target APY of ~20%.
2
Treasury Reserve — the remainder is held permanently in the Inclawbate treasury. This INCLAWNCH never returns to the open market.

Every partner token added to Inclawbate is another fee source. More tokens = more fees = more INCLAWNCH bought daily = more distributed to stakers + more locked in treasury forever.

Why INCLAWNCH is a store of value: Every trade across every partner token creates buy pressure on INCLAWNCH — but only a portion ever returns to circulation through UBI. The rest is permanently removed. The more the ecosystem grows, the more INCLAWNCH is taken off the market. The floor rises with every partner added.
