Why CLAWS is a store of value: Every trade across every partner token creates buy pressure on CLAWS — but only a portion returns to circulation through UBI. The treasury has no operational costs requiring a sell, so most of it stays off the market. The more the ecosystem grows, the more CLAWS is bought daily. The floor rises with every partner added.
