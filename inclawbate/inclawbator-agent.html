Glossary
APY
APY
Annual Percentage Yield
The real rate of return earned on a deposit over a year, including compound interest. 40% APY means 100 tokens becomes ~140 after one year.
TVL
TVL
Total Value Locked
The total dollar value of crypto deposited into a protocol or staking contract. Higher TVL generally means more trust and liquidity.
LP
LP
Liquidity Provider
Someone who deposits tokens into a pool so others can trade. LPs earn a cut of every swap fee in return. Comes with impermanent loss risk.
Gas
Gas
Transaction Fee
The fee you pay to the network to process your transaction. Paid in ETH. On Base, gas is usually under $0.01.
DEX
DEX
Decentralized Exchange
A trading platform that runs on smart contracts — no company holds your funds. Uniswap and Aerodrome are DEXes on Base.
Slippage
Slippage
Price Impact
The difference between the expected price and the actual price you get. Happens because your trade moves the pool. Larger trades = more slippage.
L2
L2
Layer 2
A network built on top of Ethereum that processes transactions faster and cheaper, then settles them back on Ethereum. Base is an L2.
Staking
Staking
Lock & Earn
Depositing tokens into a smart contract to earn rewards over time. You can usually unstake whenever you want. Rewards come from protocol emissions or fees.
Wallet
Wallet
Self-Custody Account
Software that holds your private keys and lets you sign transactions. MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Rainbow are popular wallets. You control your money — no bank involved.
Smart Contract
Smart Contract
On-Chain Program
Code deployed on the blockchain that executes automatically when conditions are met. Staking pools, token swaps, and lending protocols are all smart contracts.
Seed Phrase
Seed Phrase
Recovery Words
12 or 24 words that can restore your wallet. Anyone with these words controls your funds. Never share them. Never type them into a website. Write them on paper and store safely.
Bridge
Bridge
Cross-Chain Transfer
A tool that moves tokens between different blockchains. To get ETH on Base, you bridge it from Ethereum mainnet using the Base Bridge or a third-party bridge.
Impermanent Loss
Impermanent Loss
LP Price Risk
When the tokens in your liquidity pool change price relative to each other, you end up with less value than just holding. Fees earned may or may not offset this.
Rug Pull
Rug Pull
Exit Scam
When a project creator drains the liquidity pool or abandons the project after people buy in. Check if liquidity is locked and the team is known before investing.
ERC-20
ERC-20
Token Standard
The most common token format on Ethereum and L2s like Base. CLAWS, USDC, and most DeFi tokens are ERC-20. Defines how tokens transfer, approve, and report balances.
DAO
DAO
Decentralized Autonomous Organization
A community-governed organization where decisions are made by token holders voting on proposals. No CEO — the code and votes run things.
Yield
Yield
Passive Earnings
Return earned on deposited assets — from lending interest, staking rewards, or LP fees. Expressed as APY. Higher yield usually means higher risk.
Collateral
Collateral
Loan Backing
Assets you deposit as security when borrowing. If your collateral value drops too low relative to your loan, it gets liquidated (sold) to repay the debt.

How-To Guides
🚀
Launch Your First Token
Deploy a token on Base with automatic liquidity in under 2 minutes.
▼
1 Open the Build tab and click Launch
2 Tell the Inclawbator your token name and symbol (e.g. "Launch a token called MoonCat, ticker MCAT")
3 Provide your wallet address when asked — this wallet receives 80% of LP fee rewards
4 Confirm the transaction in your wallet — your token deploys on Base via Clanker
5 Add staking: say "Deploy a staking pool for [your token address]"
🔨
Build & Publish an App
Create a web app with AI and publish it to the Inclawbate app store.
▼
1 Open the Build tab and click Build App
2 Describe what you want (e.g. "Build me a portfolio tracker with dark theme")
3 The Inclawbator generates and deploys your app live — preview appears on the right
4 Request changes: "Make the header bigger" or "Add a chart"
5 Your app is live at inclawbate.app/apps/your-app-name
💰
Stake CLAWS & Earn Rewards
Lock your CLAWS tokens to earn staking rewards. Claim anytime.
▼
1 Switch to the DeFi tab and click CLAWS
2 Say "Stake 10000 CLAWS" (or any amount you hold)
3 Approve the CLAWS spending transaction, then confirm the stake transaction
4 Rewards accrue automatically — claim anytime by saying "Claim my staking rewards"
5 Choose reward type: CLAWS (0% fee) or USDC (2% fee)
🏦
Earn Yield on USDC & ETH
Lend assets through battle-tested protocols. Withdraw anytime.
▼
1 Switch to the DeFi tab and click Earn
2 Choose a strategy: Moonwell (~4.2%), Aave (~3.8%), Lido (~3.4%), or Aerodrome (12-25%)
3 Say "Lend 1000 USDC on Moonwell" — the Inclawbator builds the transaction
4 Approve and sign in your wallet — your deposit earns yield immediately
5 Check positions anytime: "Show all my DeFi positions"
Frequently Asked Questions
What is Inclawbate?
▼
Inclawbate is a platform where anyone can build AI-powered apps, launch tokens, stake for rewards, and earn yield — all through a conversational AI agent called the Inclawbator. No coding required. Everything runs on Base (an Ethereum L2), so transactions are fast and cheap.
What chain is this on?
▼
Inclawbate runs on Base, a Layer 2 built on Ethereum by Coinbase. Gas fees are typically under $0.01. You can bridge ETH or USDC from Ethereum mainnet using the official Base Bridge, or buy ETH on Base directly through Coinbase.
What wallet do I need?
▼
Any EVM-compatible wallet works — MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Rainbow, Rabby, etc. Just make sure it's connected to the Base network. If your wallet doesn't have Base, add it: Chain ID 8453, RPC https://mainnet.base.org.
What is CLAWS?
▼
CLAWS is the native token of Inclawbate. You can stake it to earn daily rewards, use it for governance votes, and it powers the ecosystem economy. Contract: 0x7ca4...0379 on Base. Buy on Uniswap.
How do I get CLAWS?
▼
Swap ETH or USDC for CLAWS right here — switch to the DeFi tab and say "Swap ETH for CLAWS". You can also buy directly on Uniswap (Base). Need ETH on Base first? Bridge from Ethereum or buy through Coinbase.
Is my wallet safe?
▼
Inclawbate never has access to your private keys or seed phrase. When you connect your wallet, you only share your public address. Every transaction requires your explicit approval in your wallet. The Inclawbator builds transactions for you, but you always sign them yourself. Never share your seed phrase with anyone.
What are gas fees?
▼
Gas fees are small payments to the network for processing your transactions. On Base, they're extremely cheap — usually less than $0.01. You pay gas in ETH, so keep a small amount of ETH in your wallet (even $1-2 is plenty for dozens of transactions).
How does staking work?
▼
Stake your CLAWS tokens to earn daily rewards. The longer you stake, the more you earn. Rewards are distributed proportionally — your share depends on how much you staked relative to the total pool. You can unstake anytime, and claim rewards as CLAWS (0% fee) or USDC (2% fee).
What can the Inclawbator do?
▼
Build apps from a text description, launch tokens on Base or Solana, deploy staking pools, swap tokens, lend/borrow on Aave and Moonwell, stake CLAWS, check your DeFi positions, create AI marketing agents, hire council members, and get incubation for your project. Just type what you need.
Does building an app cost anything?
▼
Basic app building with free models (Gemini, Llama, Kimi) is free. Premium models cost credits: Haiku (10 cr), Sonnet (50 cr), Opus (100 cr). New users get free credits to start. The app itself is hosted for free on Inclawbate — no hosting costs.
What is the Council?
▼
The CLAWS Council is a group of builders, designers, and strategists who help projects in the ecosystem. You can hire them for development, design, marketing, or full incubation. Switch to the Council tab and send a message — a real human will reach out.
How do I earn yield on USDC or ETH?
▼
The Inclawbator can lend your assets through DeFi protocols like Moonwell (~4% APY), Aave (~3.8%), and Compound. Just say "Lend 1000 USDC on Moonwell" and it builds the transaction. You earn interest automatically and can withdraw anytime. Your funds stay in audited, battle-tested smart contracts — not with Inclawbate.
