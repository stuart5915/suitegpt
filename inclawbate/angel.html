What is the Angel NFT?

A non-dilutable founding badge on Base. Only 828 can ever exist — one per snapshot wallet. You can’t buy your way in. You had to be here.

  • Max supply: 828 (capped by snapshot)
  • 1 per wallet — no exceptions
  • Tradeable on OpenSea, Element, etc.
  • 5% royalty on every resale
  • Proof you were a founding supporter

What happens to the INCLAWNCH?

All INCLAWNCH collected from minting gets sold for ETH, which is used to deepen CLAWS liquidity. Every mint directly strengthens the token floor.
