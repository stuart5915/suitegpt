What is the Angel NFT?

The Inclawbate Angel NFT is a badge for the original 833 snapshot holders who supported INCLAWNCH. To mint, you send your snapshot INCLAWNCH balance to the contract. In return, you receive a non-transferable founding badge on Base.

  • Max supply: 833 (one per snapshot wallet)
  • Cost: your snapshot INCLAWNCH balance
  • Tradeable on secondary markets
  • Collected INCLAWNCH funds CLAWS ecosystem growth
