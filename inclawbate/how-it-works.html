Governance
CLAWS-Weighted Voting
Every CLAWS holder gets a say in how treasury value is allocated. More CLAWS = more weight. All votes are on-chain and publicly verifiable.
🏛 How It Works
  • Who votes: Anyone holding or staking CLAWS
  • Weight: Your CLAWS balance (wallet + staked) = your vote weight
  • Transparent: Every vote is signed on-chain and publicly visible below
  • Real-time: The synthesis bar updates live as votes come in
💰 Treasury
  • Where: Multisig Safe on Base — requires multiple signatures
  • Auditable: Anyone can verify the balance on-chain
  • Allocation: Driven by the community synthesis below
  • Execution: Active contributors execute what the community votes for
