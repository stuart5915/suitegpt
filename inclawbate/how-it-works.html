Governance
The CLAWS Council
A group of committed, active contributors who govern how ecosystem revenue gets distributed. Think board of directors — holders vote, but the council runs the show.
👑 Council Members
  • Who: Active builders, contributors, and committed community leaders
  • How: Invited based on contribution, not just token holdings
  • Power: Majority voting weight on all allocation decisions
  • Execution: Multisig signers — proposals require enough signatures to execute on-chain
🏛 CLAWS Holders
  • Who: Anyone holding or staking CLAWS
  • How: Vote on proposals through snapshot or on-chain voting
  • Power: Minority voting weight — a voice, not the final say
  • Transparency: Can attend weekly meetings, see all treasury activity
⚖ Voting Weight
