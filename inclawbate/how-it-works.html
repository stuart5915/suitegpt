The Flywheel

How Every Project Fuels CLAWS Forever

When a project launches through Inclawbate, it creates a permanent reward loop. Trading fees auto-buy CLAWS and deposit them as staking rewards. As long as people trade, stakers earn.

1

Project Launches Token

ERC-20 deployed on Base via Clanker v4. Fee split auto-configured: 80% creator / 20% Inclawbate.

2

People Trade the Token

Token listed on Uniswap. Every buy/sell generates a 1.2% LP trading fee on Clanker.

↓
3

20% Auto-Buys CLAWS

Inclawbate's 20% fee share is automatically swapped to CLAWS on the open market. Creates constant buy pressure.

4

CLAWS Deposited as Rewards

Purchased CLAWS are deposited into the project's staking pool. Stakers start earning immediately.

↓
5

Community Stakes & Earns

Token holders stake and earn CLAWS rewards. More staking = stronger community = more trading.

6

Ecosystem Grows

Every new project adds more volume, more CLAWS buying, more rewards. The flywheel accelerates with each launch.

Value Management

How the Treasury Grows Value

Collected fees don't sit idle. The platform actively manages value through DeFi strategies.

💰

Concentrated LP

CLAWS/ETH and CLAWS/USDC liquidity positions on Aerodrome with tight ranges for maximum fee yield.

🏦

Lending / Borrowing

Supply assets on Aave to earn yield. Borrow against positions to deploy capital without selling.

🔄

Yield Recycling

All yield generated flows back into CLAWS staking rewards and ecosystem operations. Compounding value.
