Governance
The CLAWS Council
Active contributors who govern how the Treasury Safe gets allocated. They don't touch daily ops — they decide where the surplus goes.
👑 Council Members
  • Who: Active builders and contributors — the people in the trenches
  • How you get on: Existing council recognizes your contribution and adds you
  • Execution: Multisig signers on the Treasury Safe
🏛 CLAWS Holders
  • Who: Anyone holding or staking CLAWS
  • Voting: CLAWS tokens = vote weight — more skin in the game, louder voice
  • Transparency: Can attend weekly meetings, see all treasury activity
  • Power: Majority vote weight — stakers drive allocation decisions
