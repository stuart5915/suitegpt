Introducing the Inclawbator: Build Anything. Launch Anything. Earn Everything.

Mar 4, 2026 · 18 min read

The idea behind Inclawbate — providing real tools and real incubation for anyone — has been years in the making. The AI agent space is loud. Noisy. Crowded with vaporware and hype cycles. Cutting through all of that, we found the foundation of what Inclawbate will be moving forward: a full-stack incubation platform that takes a project from zero to launched, staked, and earning.

We learned a lot along the way, and it all manifests here. Token launching, staking pools, an AI-powered Build Studio, hype agents, a credits economy, and more. All of it is live. All of it runs on CLAWS.

What is the Inclawbator?

The Inclawbator is an all-in-one platform for creating, launching, and growing crypto projects on Base. Think of it as a launchpad, staking platform, app builder, and agent marketplace — merged into one interface.

Every tool is designed to be permissionless. Anyone can launch a token. Anyone can deploy staking. Anyone can build and publish an app. There are no whitelists, no applications, and no gatekeepers. The platform makes money when you make money — aligned incentives from day one.

  • Launch — Deploy an ERC-20 token with built-in liquidity in one click
  • Stake — Deploy a staking pool for your token in one click, free
  • Build — AI-powered app builder with three model tiers
  • Earn — Trading fees, staking rewards, app monetization — all in CLAWS
  • Grow — AI hype agents, skills marketplace, and a human talent directory

One-Click Token Launching

Launching a token shouldn't take a Solidity engineer and $5K in audit fees. With the Inclawbator, you fill in a name, ticker, and description, hit Launch, and your token is deployed via Clanker with automatic liquidity on Uniswap V3 — all on Base.

Every launch has a flat 1% swap fee on trading volume, split 80/20: 80% goes directly to the token creator in ETH (visible on your Clanker dashboard and your Inclawbate dashboard), and 20% goes to the Inclawbate protocol — used to purchase CLAWS for the CLAWS staking pool. No hidden fees. No VC allocation.

Dev Allocation Tiers

By default, 100% of supply goes to LP. But founders who want a dev allocation can burn CLAWS to unlock one:

  • 1% supply — Burn a small amount of CLAWS
  • 5% supply — Moderate CLAWS burn
  • 10% supply — Large CLAWS burn

This is a deflationary mechanism: the CLAWS are burned forever, reducing circulating supply every time someone launches with a dev allocation.

One-click token launch with optional dev allocation tiers

One-Click Staking Pools

Every token launched through the Inclawbator can deploy a staking pool in one click — on-chain, free of charge. No configuration needed. Hit deploy, and your holders can start staking immediately.

Staking pools use the same battle-tested contract that powers CLAWS staking itself. Pool creators can deposit CLAWS tokens as rewards for their LP holders at any time, and rewards are distributed proportionally based on stake weight.

Zero cost to deploy. Other platforms charge $500–$2,000 for staking infrastructure. On the Inclawbator, it's free.

Auto-deployed staking pools for every launched token

The CLAWS Flywheel

CLAWS is the native token of the entire Inclawbate ecosystem. It doesn't just sit in your wallet — it's the fuel that powers every piece of the platform.

Here's how the flywheel works:

  1. Tokens trade — Every swap on an Inclawbator token generates a 1% fee
  2. 80% → Creator — The creator earns 80% of swap fees in ETH, visible on their Clanker and Inclawbate dashboards
  3. 20% → Protocol — Inclawbate's share is used to buy CLAWS on the open market
  4. CLAWS → Staking rewards — Purchased CLAWS are deposited into the CLAWS staking pool

The more tokens that get launched and traded, the more CLAWS gets bought for staking rewards. Creators earn from day one, and CLAWS stakers benefit from every trade across the ecosystem. It's a self-reinforcing cycle.

Build Studio

Build Studio is an AI-powered app builder that lets anyone create, publish, and monetize web apps — no coding required. Describe what you want, pick a model, and Build Studio generates a working application.

Three Model Tiers

  • Haiku — 10 credits per build. Fast, great for simple tools and landing pages.
  • Sonnet — 50 credits per build. Balanced power for most apps.
  • Opus — 100 credits per build. Maximum intelligence for complex applications.

The 8 Pillars

Every Build Studio app is built around 8 pillars: Design, Data, Logic, Auth, Payments, Hosting, SEO, and Analytics. The AI handles all of them.

Once built, apps publish to a custom slug on the Inclawbate app store. Set a price in CLAWS (or make it free), and you earn every time someone unlocks your app.

Build Studio — describe it, build it, publish it

Credits System

Credits are the internal currency that powers Build Studio, AI agents, and X-Search. They're designed to be dead simple: ~$0.005 per credit.

Two Ways to Get Credits

  • Card Subscriptions — Three tiers via Stripe:
    • Starter: $6/month — 1,200 credits
    • Builder: $19/month — 3,800 credits
    • Pro: $55/month — 11,000 credits
  • CLAWS Deposits — Deposit CLAWS tokens to your account and receive credits at market rate. No subscription needed. Pay-as-you-go with crypto.
Credits pricing — card or crypto, your choice

App Store & Monetization

Every app built through Build Studio (or submitted by developers) lives in the Inclawbate App Store. It's a browsable, searchable marketplace where users can discover tools, games, dashboards, and utilities — all powered by CLAWS.

How It Works

  • Browse & upvote — Discover apps, see what's trending
  • Use for free — Many apps are completely free — no CLAWS needed, anyone can jump in and use them
  • Unlock premium — Some creators set a CLAWS price for their app
  • Tip — Send CLAWS directly to any app creator
  • Fork — See an app you like? Fork it, improve it, republish it

Creators set their own pricing — free, one-time unlock, or whatever they want. Inclawbate takes zero cut on app sales — 100% goes to the creator.

The Inclawbate App Store — browse, unlock, tip, fork

AI Hype Agents COMING SOON

Every app on the platform can have an AI hype agent attached to it. These are autonomous agents that post to X (Twitter) on behalf of your project — generating buzz, engagement, and awareness around the clock.

Each app starts with 10 free agent credits. After that, anyone (the creator, a fan, a whale) can fund more credits to keep the agent posting. The agent writes contextual, on-brand tweets based on your app's description and category.

Community-funded marketing. Your agent doesn't stop when you run out of credits — anyone who believes in your project can top it up.

AI Hype Agents — autonomous marketing for any app

Staking Universe: 3 Pool Types

Inclawbate doesn't have one staking pool. It has an entire staking universe, with three distinct pool types serving different purposes:

1. CLAWS Staking

The flagship. Stake CLAWS, earn CLAWS. Rewards come from the trading fee flywheel — every time an Inclawbator token is traded, a portion of fees buys CLAWS and deposits it into the staking pool. APY fluctuates based on trading volume across all Inclawbator tokens.

2. Partner Pools

Cross-ecosystem staking. Stake Token A, earn Token B. Enables partnerships between different projects on the platform.

3. Inclawbator Pools

One-click staking deployment for any Inclawbator-launched token. Free to deploy.

Three pool types — CLAWS Staking, Partner, and Inclawbator

Incubation Tiers

Not every project needs the same level of support. The Inclawbator offers three incubation tiers:

Permissionless

Anyone, anytime. Launch a token, get staking, list in the app store. No approval needed. This is the default for everyone.

Lite Incubation ($5–$50)

Hands-on support from the Inclawbate team at an accessible price point. Depending on complexity, you get featured placement, social promotion, strategy guidance, and help getting your app or token in front of the right audience. Designed for projects that want a boost without giving up control.

Super Incubation

Full-service incubation. This can be a fixed-price engagement or a deeper partnership where we work together to integrate revenue models, custom staking configurations, marketing campaigns, and more. Super-incubated projects get listed in our partners section, partnership introductions, and priority access to new platform features.

Three tiers — from permissionless to full-service incubation
