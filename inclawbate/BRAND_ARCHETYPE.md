# Inclawbate Brand Archetype — Visual & Prompt Reference

Use this file as the source of truth for generating images, videos, thumbnails, social media visuals, and AI image prompts across the Inclawbate ecosystem.

---

## Brand Essence

Inclawbate is a Web3 platform on Base where anyone builds apps with AI, launches tokens, and earns. The vibe is **crypto-native but approachable** — not sterile corporate, not chaotic degen. Think: a futuristic workshop where builders create things, powered by a lobster mascot with attitude.

**Tagline energy:** "Anyone Can Build. Everyone Gets Paid."

---

## The Lobster Mascot

The lobster (the "Inclawbator") is the central brand character. It's not cute/kawaii — it's confident, slightly mischievous, builder-energy.

**Prompt keywords for the lobster:**
- Red/coral lobster character, stylized, anthropomorphic
- Confident posture, builder energy, slightly cocky grin
- Can hold tools: laptop, wrench, rocket, token coin, phone
- Eyes are expressive — curious, determined, playful
- NOT realistic/photographic — stylized illustration or 3D render
- Can wear accessories: hard hat, hoodie, headphones, VR goggles

**Lobster color:** Coral red `#e5533d` with darker depth tones `#d4403a`, `#c9442e`, `#b83c28`

## Visual Style Rules

### DO
- **Dark backgrounds** — near-black with subtle noise/grain texture
- **Neon glow effects** — coral and teal glows on edges, buttons, icons
- **Futuristic but warm** — not cold/sterile, has personality
- **Depth and layers** — floating cards, glass morphism, subtle shadows
- **Crypto aesthetic** — blockchain nodes, token coins, charts going up, Base chain logo
- **Builder tools** — code editors, app screens, construction metaphors
- **Organic + tech fusion** — lobster claws gripping circuit boards, coral reef meets server farm

### DON'T
- No bright/white backgrounds (always dark)
- No stock photo energy (no handshakes, no suits, no generic "team meeting")
- No clip art or flat corporate illustration
- No pure blue (blue is not in the palette — use teal/seafoam instead)
- No text in images unless specifically requested (AI image generators are bad at text)
- No realistic photography of people (use illustrated/stylized characters or abstract)
- No busy/cluttered compositions — clean with breathing room

## Scene Templates

Use these as starting points for different content types:

### App Spotlight
> Dark background with subtle grid pattern. A glowing app interface floats center-frame, slightly tilted in 3D perspective. Coral and teal accent lights illuminate the edges. The Inclawbate lobster mascot peeks from behind the app screen, giving a thumbs-up claw. Futuristic, clean, Web3 aesthetic.

### Builder Shoutout
> Dark moody workspace scene. A stylized character (or the lobster) sits at a glowing desk with multiple floating holographic screens showing code and app UIs. Coral neon accents. Tools and tokens scattered on the desk. Vibe: late-night builder grinding. Isometric or slight top-down angle.

### Token / DeFi Update
> Abstract visualization of a token ecosystem. A coral-colored coin with claw marks floats in center, surrounded by orbiting data streams in seafoam teal. Dark space background with subtle star field. Yield arrows pointing upward. Charts and numbers rendered as holographic overlays. Clean and premium.

### Weekly Recap
> Split-panel mosaic showing multiple mini app screens, token charts, and builder avatars, all connected by glowing coral lines on a dark background. Energy: "look at everything that shipped." Top-down dashboard perspective with depth-of-field blur at edges.

### How-To / Tutorial
> Clean dark UI mockup showing a step-by-step process. Numbered steps glow in coral. The lobster mascot points at the current step with a claw. Minimal, instructional, but stylish. Think: futuristic IKEA manual meets crypto.

### Community / Vibes
> Crowd scene of stylized characters (or multiple lobsters) in a neon-lit crypto lounge. Coral and teal lighting. Some are on phones, some on laptops, one is launching a token on a big screen. Energy: Web3 co-working space party. Warm despite the dark palette.

### Incubation CTA
> The Inclawbate lobster in a dramatic pose — perhaps emerging from a glowing portal or standing atop a stack of apps it built. Coral energy radiating outward. Dark background. Epic but not over-the-top. Think: "bring me your ideas" energy. Confident, powerful.

## Image Prompt Formula

When generating image prompts for content, follow this structure:

```
[Subject/scene description], [Inclawbate visual style keywords], [color palette reference], [mood/energy], [composition notes]
```

**Always include these style anchors:**
- "dark background, near-black (#06060b)"
- "coral red (#e5533d) and seafoam teal (#4db6ac) accent lighting"
- "futuristic Web3 aesthetic"
- "clean composition, no text in image"
- "subtle grain/noise texture"

**Example prompt:**
> A stylized coral-red lobster character confidently holding a glowing smartphone showing a crypto app interface, dark near-black background (#06060b) with subtle grid pattern, coral (#e5533d) and seafoam teal (#4db6ac) neon edge lighting, futuristic Web3 aesthetic, clean composition with breathing room, subtle grain texture, 1:1 aspect ratio

## Video / Animation Notes

For any video or animated content:
- **Transitions:** Smooth ease-out (200-400ms), no jarring cuts
- **Motion:** Subtle floating/breathing on idle elements, glows that pulse slowly
- **Intros:** Dark → coral glow reveals the scene
- **Text overlays:** Nunito font, warm white on dark, minimal words
- **Music vibe:** Lo-fi electronic, chill but forward-moving, NOT hype/EDM
