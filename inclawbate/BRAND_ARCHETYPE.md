# Inclawbate Brand Archetype — Visual & Prompt Reference

Source of truth for generating images, videos, thumbnails, and social media visuals.

---

## Brand Essence

Inclawbate exists to **Love God and Love Others** — manifested through technology, stewardship, and incubation. It's a self-sustaining ecosystem that generates, manages, and distributes real value — for builders, for holders, for people in need.

**Tagline:** "Anyone Can Build. Everyone Gets Paid."

**Core identity:** Not a tech company. Not a crypto project. A **stewardship engine** — using AI, DeFi, and human coordination to help people flourish and direct surplus toward philanthropy. Jesus is Lord. Build accordingly.

---

## Visual Identity — Human-Centered

The visuals center **real humans** benefiting from what Inclawbate builds. The lobster remains as a brand mark/logo (favicon, icon, corner badge) but is NOT the protagonist of images. Humans are.

### Who We Show

- **Builders** — people creating apps, launching projects, learning new skills
- **Families** — using tools like FoodVitals, managing household value, hedging against inflation
- **Mission teams** — coordinating energy and resources for philanthropy through Inclawbate tools
- **Communities** — gathering, deciding together, stewarding shared resources
- **Individuals** — checking their staking rewards, watching their value grow, feeling empowered

### What We Show Them Doing

- Using a real app on a phone or laptop (FoodVitals, staking dashboard, app builder)
- Receiving value — a notification, a reward, a transfer that changes their day
- Coordinating — a small team planning a mission trip using Inclawbate tools
- Building — typing an idea and watching it become real
- Resting — knowing their treasury is self-sustaining, their stake is growing, their savings are protected

## Photography & Illustration Style

### ALWAYS
- **Warm, natural lighting** — golden hour, soft sunlight through windows, morning light
- **Bright, clean compositions** — airy, hopeful, abundant
- **Real-world settings** — kitchens, offices, churches, parks, community centers, developing-world villages
- **Diverse humans** — different ages, ethnicities, contexts. The mission is for everyone
- **Grounded and tangible** — show real screens, real tools, real impact
- **Emotional truth** — a mother's relief, a builder's pride, a family's security
- **The lobster as a subtle accent** — a small logo on a screen, a sticker on a laptop, an icon in a notification. Present but not dominant

### NEVER
- Dark/neon/cyberpunk backgrounds
- The lobster as the main character in scenes
- 3D cartoon renders as the primary style
- Stock photography that feels generic or posed
- Dystopian, cold, or clinical vibes
- Text burned into images
- Overly technical/chart-heavy compositions

## Scene Templates by Content Pillar

### 1. App Spotlight
> A person sitting at a kitchen table, morning light through the window, looking at a clean app interface on their phone. Their expression is one of genuine usefulness — "this actually helps me." A laptop nearby with the Inclawbate logo subtly visible as a sticker. Warm, editorial photography style, soft depth of field, 1:1.

### 2. Builder Shoutout
> A young person at a desk, hands on keyboard, screen showing an app they just built using Inclawbate's builder. The room is bright and tidy. A coffee mug, a notebook with sketches. Their face shows quiet pride — they made something real. Natural window light, warm tones, editorial feel, 1:1.

### 3. DeFi / Stewardship Update
> A family gathered at a dining table, one parent showing the other a phone screen — their staking rewards, their savings growing. The kitchen is warm, well-lit, real. A child draws nearby. The feeling: financial peace. Soft focus background, warm golden tones, 1:1.

> Two people at a shared desk, one showing the other how to use a tool on screen. The teacher points, the learner leans in with genuine curiosity. Bright room, books on a shelf, a plant in the corner. Mentoring energy. Natural light, warm composition, 1:1.

### 5. Community / Engagement
> A diverse group around a long table — some with laptops, some with notebooks. A community meeting, a council session, a planning meeting for something that matters. Golden hour light through large windows. Warmth, belonging, purpose. Wide shot, 1:1.

### 6. Incubation / Launch
> A person at their laptop, screen glowing with a freshly deployed app. They lean back with a smile — it's live. Behind them, a window showing a bright day outside. The beginning of something. Warm, hopeful, real. Natural light, 1:1.

### 7. Philanthropy / Mission
> A small team in a developing-world community center, using tablets and phones to coordinate resources. Local people are present, involved, empowered — not passive recipients. Warm, authentic, dignified. Natural light, documentary photography feel, 1:1.

### 8. Weekly Recap / Milestone
> Overhead shot of a team's workspace — laptops, coffee, notebooks, a whiteboard with checkmarks. The evidence of a productive week. Warm afternoon light. Not staged — lived-in and real. 1:1.

## Image Prompt Formula

Every prompt should follow this structure:

```
[Person/people] in [real-world setting], [doing something meaningful with Inclawbate tools]. [Emotional state]. [Lighting: warm natural light, golden hour, soft window light]. [Style: editorial photography, authentic, bright, warm tones]. Small Inclawbate lobster logo visible as [subtle accent — sticker, screen icon, notification badge]. 1:1 aspect ratio.
```

### Core style anchors (include in EVERY prompt):
- "warm natural lighting" or "golden hour" or "soft window light"
- Real human subject(s) as focal point
- A real-world setting (kitchen, office, community center, etc.)
- Emotional truth (relief, pride, curiosity, belonging)
- "editorial photography style" or "documentary feel"
- Bright, clean, hopeful composition
- Lobster logo as subtle accent only

## The Lobster Brand Mark

The lobster is NOT retired — it's repositioned:

- **Favicon**: Lobster icon
- **Logo**: Lobster mark + "inclawbate" wordmark
- **Subtle accent in images**: Sticker on a laptop, icon in a notification, logo on a screen
- **Standalone icon**: App store, social profile pictures, token icon
- **NOT**: The protagonist of content images, 3D rendered character scenes, mascot-centric storytelling
