## Brand Essence

Inclawbate = Web3 platform on Base where anyone builds apps with AI, launches tokens, and earns. The vibe is **crypto-native but approachable**. Not corporate. Not chaotic degen. A futuristic builder workshop powered by a lobster mascot with big personality.

**Tagline:** "Anyone Can Build. Everyone Gets Paid."

## The Inclawbate Lobster — 3D Mascot

The lobster is the CORE of all visuals. It should feel like a **consistent 3D character** — like a Pixar character dropped into a crypto world. Think: if Ratatouille's rat was a lobster who builds Web3 apps.

### Character Design

- **Species:** Anthropomorphic lobster, stands upright on tail/legs
- **Build:** Chunky, rounded, approachable proportions (not skinny/spindly)
- **Color:** Rich coral red (#e5533d) body, darker red-brown (#c9442e, #b83c28) on shell segments, belly slightly lighter
- **Eyes:** Large, round, expressive with white highlight catchlights — this is where all the personality comes from. Can be curious, excited, determined, mischievous, proud
- **Claws:** Big and prominent — they're his "hands." One claw often holds something (laptop, phone, token, tool). Claws have visible pincer tips in darker red
- **Shell:** Segmented body with visible plates, smooth but textured like real lobster shell with a subtle glossy sheen
- **Legs:** 6 small legs on the underside, usually just suggested/visible, not the focus
- **Antennae:** Two long antennae that curve back, can express emotion (perked up = excited, drooped = thoughtful)
- **Tail fan:** Visible when seen from side/back, fan-shaped

### Character Personality

- **Confident** — stands tall, chest out, one claw on hip
- **Builder energy** — always making something, tinkering, launching
- **Slightly cocky grin** — knows he's good at what he does
- **Approachable** — not intimidating, you'd want to hang out with this lobster
- **NOT cute/kawaii** — not a baby character. Think adult professional with swagger
- **NOT scary/aggressive** — not a monster, not a villain

### 3D Render Style

**THIS IS CRITICAL — all lobster images should match this style:**

- **3D rendered, NOT flat illustration, NOT 2D cartoon, NOT pixel art**
- **Smooth, polished surfaces** with subsurface scattering on the shell (like high-end Blender/Octane render)
- **Soft ambient lighting** from below/behind with strong rim lighting in coral and teal
- **Depth of field** — subject sharp, background slightly blurred
- **Semi-realistic materials** — the shell looks like real lobster shell but stylized, eyes are cartoony
- **Dark cinematic backgrounds** — never bright, never white
- **Volumetric lighting** — light rays, god rays, atmospheric haze
- Think: **Fortnite character quality** meets **crypto neon aesthetic**

## Mandatory Style Rules

### ALWAYS
- Dark backgrounds (#06060b to #0d0d1a range)
- 3D rendered look (not flat, not illustrated, not pixel art)
- The lobster mascot as the focal point (or at least present)
- Coral + teal neon accent lighting
- Cinematic composition with depth
- Glossy/polished materials on the lobster shell
- Rim lighting / back lighting for drama
- Clean, uncluttered composition

### NEVER
- White or bright backgrounds
- Flat 2D illustration style
- Stock photography
- Realistic human characters
- Text burned into the image (AI can't do text well)
- Pure blue anything (use teal/seafoam instead)
- Cluttered busy compositions
- Cute/baby/chibi style
- Horror/dark/scary vibes

## Image Prompt Formula

Every prompt should follow this structure:

```
3D rendered Inclawbate lobster mascot (coral red anthropomorphic lobster character with glossy shell, large expressive eyes, prominent claws) in [POSE], [SCENE/ACTION], dark background (#06060b), coral (#e5533d) and seafoam teal (#4db6ac) neon accent lighting, volumetric lighting, cinematic depth of field, Octane render quality, polished 3D animation style, 1:1 aspect ratio
```

### Core style anchors (include in EVERY prompt):
- "3D rendered" or "Octane render quality"
- "Inclawbate lobster mascot" or "coral red anthropomorphic lobster"
- "dark background"
- "coral and teal neon lighting"
- "cinematic depth of field"
- "polished 3D animation style"
