Creator Flow

The complete journey from sign-up to first payout.

1. Sign Up & Age Verify

Creator creates an account with email or OAuth. Completes mandatory 18+ age verification via government ID check. This is required before accessing any platform features.

Supabase Auth ID Verification API

2. Set Up Profile

Creator chooses a display name, writes a bio, uploads a banner, and sets their subscription price ($2–$50/month). They also configure their payout method (bank, ETH wallet, or USDC wallet).

Supabase DB

3. Generate AI Content

Creator opens the AI Studio and describes the character/scene they want to generate. The system runs the prompt through a safety filter, generates the image via AI, then runs a post-generation content check. Approved content goes to the creator's draft library.

Flux / DALL-E API Safety Filter Content Storage

4. Publish Content

Creator selects images from their draft library, adds captions, and publishes to their feed. They choose whether each post is free (visible to all) or subscriber-only (gated). Published content appears in the discover feed and on their profile.

Supabase DB CDN

5. Earn Revenue

When subscribers pay for their monthly subscription or send tips, 80% goes to the creator's balance (85% for Pro creators). The creator can view real-time earnings in their dashboard.

Payment Processor

6. Withdraw Earnings

Creator requests a payout anytime. Funds are sent to their configured method — bank account (ACH), ETH wallet, or USDC wallet. Minimum withdrawal: $20. Processing time: 1–3 business days for bank, instant for crypto.

CCBill Payout ETH / USDC Transfer

Subscriber Flow

How subscribers discover, subscribe, and access content.

1. Sign Up & Age Verify

Same as creators — email or OAuth sign-up with mandatory 18+ age verification. Subscribers cannot see any content until verified.

Supabase Auth ID Verification API

2. Browse the Discover Feed

Subscribers browse trending creators and free preview content. The discovery algorithm surfaces popular and new creators based on engagement, tags, and subscriber preferences.

Recommendation Engine

3. Subscribe to a Creator

When a subscriber finds a creator they like, they hit "Subscribe" and enter payment. They can pay with credit/debit card (via CCBill) or send ETH/USDC directly. Subscription auto-renews monthly.

CCBill Crypto Payments

4. Access Exclusive Content

Once subscribed, all gated content from that creator unlocks. The subscriber sees it in their personalized feed alongside content from other creators they follow.

CDN (Gated Access)

5. Tip & Request

Subscribers can send one-time tips to creators or pay for custom content requests (pay-per-view). Tips go directly to the creator's balance at the same 80/20 split.

Payment Processor

6. Manage Subscriptions

Subscribers can view, pause, or cancel any subscription from their account settings. No lock-in, cancel anytime.

Subscription Manager

Content Safety Pipeline

Every piece of content passes through multiple safety checks before reaching subscribers.

Step 1

Prompt Filter

Creator's text prompt is scanned for prohibited terms and concepts before image generation begins. Blocks attempts to generate minors, real people, or illegal content.

Step 2

AI Generation

Approved prompts are sent to the image generation model. The model itself has built-in safety constraints that prevent certain outputs.

Step 3

Post-Generation Scan

Generated images are scanned by a second AI model that detects prohibited content. Images that fail are rejected and never stored.

Step 4

Community Reports

Subscribers can flag content. Flagged content is reviewed by moderation. Repeated violations result in creator suspension.
