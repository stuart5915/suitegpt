# CLAWS Council

## What Is It

The CLAWS Council is the governing body of Inclawbate's treasury and ecosystem revenue. Instead of dumping 100% of revenue into general staking rewards, the council decides how value is distributed — strategically, intentionally, and transparently.

## Why It Exists

As Inclawbate builds more revenue streams (poker rake, crash house edge, token launch fees, app store, staking-as-a-service, etc.), blindly funneling everything into one staking pool is a waste. The council ensures revenue goes where it creates the most impact — whether that's rewarding stakers, funding builders, growing liquidity, or supporting philanthropy.

## How Governance Works

### Two Layers

1. **CLAWS Holders** — Any CLAWS staker can vote on proposals. Votes are token-weighted (more CLAWS = more weight). But holder votes only count for a **percentage** of the total decision.

2. **Council Members** — Active builders and contributors who carry the **majority voting weight**. Council can outweigh holder votes. These are the people in the trenches — they understand the ecosystem deeply and make informed calls.

Think shareholders vs board of directors. Holders get a voice. The council runs the show.

### Why Council Outweighs

Prevents mob-rule governance where whales who never participate can override the people actually doing the work. The council earns its weight through contribution, not just capital.

## How Rep Works

> "Rep is earned, not gamed. No points system, no formulas. The council knows who's real."

There is no automated reputation score. Council members are recognized by existing members through **contribution merit**. You earn your seat by showing up, building, and adding value. Not by gaming a leaderboard.
