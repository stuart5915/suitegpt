1. Launch Your Token

Every business needs a currency. On Inclawbate, you can launch a token in minutes through the Inclawbator.

  • Pick a name, ticker, and supply — we handle the smart contract deployment on Base
  • Pre-allocate supply — reserve tokens for your team, treasury, or community rewards before launch
  • Automatic liquidity — your token is immediately tradeable on Uniswap
  • You own the supply — do whatever you want with it. Airdrop, sell, reward, burn

Your token is live on-chain within minutes. Not weeks. Not "pending review." Minutes.

2. Build Your App

Head to inclawbate.com/build and describe what you want. Our AI builder creates a fully functional web app — no coding required.

  • Gets its own URL — your app lives at a real, shareable link on Inclawbate
  • Embed your token — give your token utility by integrating it into your app (payments, access, rewards)
  • Listed in the app store — discoverable by every Inclawbate user
  • Iterate anytime — update your app as your business evolves

An app gives your token utility. Utility gives your token value. It's that simple.

3. Create a Stake Pool

This is where the flywheel starts spinning. Create a staking pool for your token through Inclawbate's staking infrastructure.

  • Users stake your token and earn CLAWS rewards
  • You fund the reward pool with CLAWS — this creates an incentive for people to buy and hold your token
  • TVL builds social proof — when people see a staking pool with real value locked, they take your project seriously
  • Stakers become holders — they're invested in your success now

The Flywheel

Here's how these pieces feed each other:

  1. Launch your token on the Inclawbator
  2. Buy some yourself — you believe in your project, show it
  3. Fund a staking pool with CLAWS rewards — now people have a reason to buy and stake your token
  4. TVL grows — other people see the pool, stake, buy more of your token
  5. Build an app that uses your token — now there's real utility
  6. Revenue from your app can feed back into the staking pool — the cycle continues
  7. Your token has a community, utility, and yield — that's a business
