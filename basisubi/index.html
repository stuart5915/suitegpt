Concentrated Liquidity 101

Why concentrated LP matters

Traditional LPs spread capital across all prices. Concentrated LPs focus it where trades happen — earning more fees with less capital. But they need active management.

TRADITIONAL LP

Spread Thin

Capital covers $0 to infinity. Most of it sits idle, far from the current price. Low capital efficiency, low fee capture.

CONCENTRATED LP

Focused Power

All capital concentrated around the current price. Up to 4000x more efficient. But if price moves out of range — you earn nothing.

How It Works

The story of your $1,000

Follow along step by step. No jargon, just what actually happens when you deposit into the Basis vault.

1

You deposit $1,000 USDC

You send USDC to the vault. You get back "bUSDC" tokens — think of them as your receipt. If the vault has $10,000 total and you deposited $1,000, you own 10%. When the vault grows, your 10% is worth more.

/// @dev Deposit USDC, receive bUSDC shares (ERC-4626)
function _deposit(
    address caller,
    address receiver,
    uint256 assets,
    uint256 shares
) internal override nonReentrant whenNotPaused {
    require(totalAssets() + assets <= depositCap, "deposit cap");
    super._deposit(caller, receiver, assets, shares);
}
2

The vault goes to work

Your USDC gets split into USDC + ETH and placed as liquidity on Aerodrome’s ETH/USDC trading pool. This means you now have ETH exposure — roughly half your deposit is converted to ETH. The position is concentrated into a tight band around the current price (say $2,375 to $2,625) for maximum fee capture.

/// @notice Deploy idle USDC into the Aerodrome LP position.
function compound() external nonReentrant {
    uint256 idle = IERC20(asset()).balanceOf(address(this));
    require(idle >= strategy.compoundMinimum, "below minimum");
    if (tokenId == 0) {
        _mintNewPosition(idle);
    } else {
        _addToPosition(idle);
    }
}
3

Traders pay you fees

Every time someone swaps ETH↔USDC on Aerodrome and the price is within your range, you earn a tiny cut of that trade. Thousands of trades happen every day — those tiny cuts add up to real yield over time.

// Fee accrual is passive. bUSDC share price rises as fees grow.
function totalAssets() public view override returns (uint256) {
    uint256 idle = IERC20(asset()).balanceOf(address(this));
    uint256 lpValue = _getLPValueInUsdc();  // principal + uncollected fees
    return idle + lpValue;
}
4

Price drifts? Auto-rebalance

If ETH’s price moves outside your range, you stop earning. The vault detects this, pulls everything out, re-centers the range around the new price, and starts earning again. This costs a small swap fee (0.3–0.6%). Note: as price moves, your position shifts between ETH and USDC — this is called impermanent loss. Fees earned aim to outweigh this cost.

/// @notice Re-center LP position on current tick.
function rebalance() external nonReentrant {
    require(tokenId != 0, "no position");
    // 1. Remove all liquidity
    _removeAllLiquidity();
    // 2. Collect fees + tokens
    (fees0, fees1) = _collectAll();
    // 3. Burn old NFT, consolidate to USDC
    positionManager.burn(tokenId);
    if (wethBal > 0) _swap(weth, usdc, wethBal);
    // 4. Mint new centered position
    _mintNewPosition(idle);
}
5

Withdraw anytime

Burn your bUSDC, get your USDC back — now worth more than you deposited (the fees you earned). No lock-ups, no waiting period. Withdrawals work even if the vault is paused. Your money is always accessible.

/// @dev Withdrawals are NEVER paused.
function _withdraw(
    address caller, address receiver,
    address owner, uint256 assets, uint256 shares
) internal override nonReentrant {
    uint256 idle = IERC20(asset()).balanceOf(address(this));
    if (idle < assets) {
        _removeLiquidityForUsdc(assets - idle);
    }
    super._withdraw(caller, receiver, owner, assets, shares);
}

How the Vault Works — Plain English

💡 The Big Picture

You deposit USDC. The vault takes your USDC and puts it to work on Aerodrome (the biggest exchange on Base). It provides liquidity for ETH/USDC trades — every time someone swaps ETH and USDC on Aerodrome, a small trading fee gets paid. Your money earns a cut of those fees.

The trick is concentrated liquidity — instead of spreading your money across every possible price, the vault focuses it in a tight band around where ETH is trading right now. This makes your money work much harder (earning more fees per dollar) but it means the vault needs to actively manage the position.

🎯 The Price Range

The vault picks a window of about ±5% around the current ETH price.

Example: ETH is at $2,500.
The vault covers $2,375 to $2,625.
As long as ETH stays in that window, you’re earning fees.

Why 5%? A tighter range earns more fees but needs adjusting more often. A wider range earns less but is more stable. 5% is the sweet spot — high fee capture without constant adjustments.

🔄 What Happens When ETH Moves Too Far

If ETH’s price drifts outside the window, the vault stops earning. It’s just sitting there doing nothing. That’s when a rebalance needs to happen:

1
Pull all money out of the old position
→
2
Convert everything back to USDC
→
3
Create a new position centered on where ETH is now

Anyone can trigger a rebalance — it’s not locked to the admin. There’s also a small buffer zone so it doesn’t rebalance on every tiny wiggle. Each rebalance costs about $0.50–$2 in swap fees on Base.

💰 Compounding (Putting Money to Work)

When you deposit USDC, it doesn’t go into the LP position immediately — it sits idle in the vault. Same with trading fees the vault collects. To actually put this money to work, someone calls compound:

1
Collect any earned trading fees
→
2
Swap some USDC to ETH (to match the range ratio)
→
3
Add both ETH + USDC into the LP position

Like rebalancing, anyone can call compound — it’s permissionless. It only works when the position is currently earning (in range). Won’t run if there’s less than ~$10 idle (not worth the gas).

📥 Depositing

You send USDC to the vault. You get back bUSDC tokens — think of them as your receipt.

Example: The vault has $10,000 total. You deposit $1,000.
You now own 10% of the vault.
If the vault grows to $12,000, your 10% is worth $1,200.

There’s a $25,000 deposit cap while the vault is new — this limits risk during early operation.

🏦 Withdrawing

Give back your bUSDC, get your USDC — now worth more than you put in (from the fees earned). No lock-ups, no waiting period.

If the vault doesn’t have enough USDC sitting idle, it automatically pulls from the LP position to cover your withdrawal. You don’t need to worry about this — it just works.

Withdrawals can never be paused. Even if the admin pauses the vault for safety, you can always get your money out. This is built into the smart contract and can’t be changed.

What Could Go Wrong

ETH price drops a lot: Your deposit is split between ETH and USDC. If ETH crashes, the vault ends up holding more ETH (bought on the way down) and less USDC. When you withdraw, you get back less than you put in. The trading fees earned might make up for it, but they’re not guaranteed to. This is called “impermanent loss” and it’s the main risk.

Rebalancing costs money: Every time the vault rebalances, it swaps tokens — and every swap has a small fee (~0.3%). If ETH is super volatile and the vault rebalances a lot, those small fees add up.

Smart contract bugs: The vault code uses battle-tested OpenZeppelin libraries, but no code is 100% bug-free. The admin can pause new deposits if something goes wrong, but can never lock your withdrawals.
