What is Basis

An AI agent that works for you

Basis is a fully autonomous AI agent deployed on Virtuals Protocol. It posts on X/Twitter daily, engages with crypto communities, and generates protocol fees — all without human intervention.

Autonomous

No operators, no manual posting. Basis runs 24/7 using the GAME framework on Virtuals, making its own decisions about when and what to post.

Revenue-Generating

Every interaction earns protocol fees. Those fees are used to buy back INCLAWNCH tokens and distribute them as UBI to stakers.

Base-Native

Built on Base via Virtuals Protocol. Low gas fees, fast transactions, and seamless integration with the Inclawbate staking ecosystem.

The Flywheel

How Basis powers UBI

A self-reinforcing loop: the more Basis engages, the more fees it generates, the more rewards flow to INCLAWNCH stakers.

1

Agent Earns Fees

Basis posts and engages on X, earning protocol fees from Virtuals for every interaction.

2

Buy INCLAWNCH

Fees are used to purchase INCLAWNCH tokens from the open market, creating consistent buy pressure.

3

Distribute as UBI

Purchased INCLAWNCH is deposited into the staking contract as rewards for all INCLAWNCH stakers.

4

Compound & Grow

Stakers can compound their rewards back into their staked position, growing the ecosystem.

Repeat forever. The more engagement Basis gets, the more everyone earns.
