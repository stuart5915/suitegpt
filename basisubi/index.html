How the Vault Thinks
◉ Signal Inputs
  • 01 ETH price vs 7-day and 30-day moving averages — determines trend direction
  • 02 14-day rolling volatility (std dev of daily returns) — measures market chaos
  • 03 Momentum score: consecutive up/down days weighted by magnitude
  • 04 Aerodrome LP fee APR (trailing 24h) — is providing liquidity profitable right now?
  • 05 Aave utilization rates for ETH/USDC — monitors borrow cost for leverage
◉ Market Regimes
Strong Uptrend
Price > 7d MA > 30d MA, low volatility. Market is trending up cleanly.
→ 60% Long · 25% LP · 15% Hold · 0% Short
Ranging / Mild Up
Price near moving averages, moderate vol. Sideways or slow grind up.
→ 55% LP · 20% Long · 20% Hold · 5% Short
High Volatility
Large daily swings, moving averages crossing. Uncertain direction.
→ 50% Hold · 25% LP (wide) · 12% Long · 13% Short
Strong Downtrend
Price < 7d MA < 30d MA. Clear bearish momentum.
→ 50% Short · 30% Hold · 15% LP (tight) · 5% Long
◉ Rebalancing Rules
  • ▸ Check interval: Every 4 hours the vault re-evaluates market regime and current allocation
  • ▸ Drift threshold: Only rebalance if any position drifts >10% from target allocation (avoids churning)
  • ▸ Max move: Cap each rebalance at 25% of vault value — no panic all-in/all-out
  • ▸ Gas awareness: Skip rebalance if Base gas exceeds 0.5 gwei — wait for cheaper window
  • ▸ Regime confirmation: New regime must persist for 2 consecutive checks (8 hrs) before the vault shifts allocation
  • ▸ LP range auto-adjust: Tight range (±2%) in calm markets for max fees, wide range (±8%) in volatile markets to stay in range
◉ Risk Guardrails
  • ▸ Max leverage: 2x only. Never exceeds 2x exposure on any position
  • ▸ Drawdown circuit breaker: If vault drops 15% from all-time high, exit all positions to 100% USDC hold until regime stabilizes
  • ▸ Minimum USDC buffer: Always keeps at least 10% in USDC as dry powder — never fully deployed
  • ▸ Aave health floor: Maintains >1.5 health factor on all leveraged positions — auto-deleverages if approaching liquidation
  • ▸ Slippage cap: Max 0.5% slippage per swap. Larger rebalances split into multiple txs
  • ▸ No oracle dependency: Uses on-chain TWAP from Aerodrome pool — resistant to flash loan manipulation

Concentrated Liquidity 101

Why concentrated LP matters

Traditional LPs spread capital across all prices. Concentrated LPs focus it where trades happen — earning more fees with less capital. But they need active management.

TRADITIONAL LP

Spread Thin

Capital covers $0 to infinity. Most of it sits idle, far from the current price. Low capital efficiency, low fee capture.

CONCENTRATED LP

Focused Power

All capital concentrated around the current price. Up to 4000x more efficient. But if price moves out of range — you earn nothing.

THE VAULT’S JOB

Auto Re-Center

Price drifts out? The vault detects it, pulls liquidity, re-centers the range, and keeps earning. That’s what Basis handles for you.

How It Works

The story of your $1,000

Follow along step by step. No jargon, just what actually happens when you deposit into the Basis vault.

1

You deposit $1,000 USDC

You send USDC to the vault. You get back "bUSDC" tokens — think of them as your receipt. If the vault has $10,000 total and you deposited $1,000, you own 10%. When the vault grows, your 10% is worth more.

/// @dev Deposit USDC, receive bUSDC shares (ERC-4626)
function _deposit(
    address caller,
    address receiver,
    uint256 assets,
    uint256 shares
) internal override nonReentrant whenNotPaused {
    require(totalAssets() + assets <= depositCap, "deposit cap");
    super._deposit(caller, receiver, assets, shares);
}
2

The vault goes to work

Your USDC gets split into USDC + ETH and placed as liquidity on Aerodrome’s ETH/USDC trading pool. This means you now have ETH exposure — roughly half your deposit is converted to ETH. The position is concentrated into a tight band around the current price (say $2,375 to $2,625) for maximum fee capture.

/// @notice Deploy idle USDC into the Aerodrome LP position.
function compound() external nonReentrant {
    uint256 idle = IERC20(asset()).balanceOf(address(this));
    require(idle >= strategy.compoundMinimum, "below minimum");
    if (tokenId == 0) {
        _mintNewPosition(idle);
    } else {
        _addToPosition(idle);
    }
}
3

Traders pay you fees

Every time someone swaps ETH↔USDC on Aerodrome and the price is within your range, you earn a tiny cut of that trade. Thousands of trades happen every day — those tiny cuts add up to real yield over time.

// Fee accrual is passive. bUSDC share price rises as fees grow.
function totalAssets() public view override returns (uint256) {
    uint256 idle = IERC20(asset()).balanceOf(address(this));
    uint256 lpValue = _getLPValueInUsdc();  // principal + uncollected fees
    return idle + lpValue;
}
4

Price drifts? Auto-rebalance

If ETH’s price moves outside your range, you stop earning. The vault detects this, pulls everything out, re-centers the range around the new price, and starts earning again. This costs a small swap fee (0.3–0.6%). Note: as price moves, your position shifts between ETH and USDC — this is called impermanent loss. Fees earned aim to outweigh this cost.

/// @notice Re-center LP position on current tick.
function rebalance() external nonReentrant {
    require(tokenId != 0, "no position");
    // 1. Remove all liquidity
    _removeAllLiquidity();
    // 2. Collect fees + tokens
    (fees0, fees1) = _collectAll();
    // 3. Burn old NFT, consolidate to USDC
    positionManager.burn(tokenId);
    if (wethBal > 0) _swap(weth, usdc, wethBal);
    // 4. Mint new centered position
    _mintNewPosition(idle);
}
5

Withdraw anytime

Burn your bUSDC, get your USDC back — now worth more than you deposited (the fees you earned). No lock-ups, no waiting period. Withdrawals work even if the vault is paused. Your money is always accessible.

/// @dev Withdrawals are NEVER paused.
function _withdraw(
    address caller, address receiver,
    address owner, uint256 assets, uint256 shares
) internal override nonReentrant {
    uint256 idle = IERC20(asset()).balanceOf(address(this));
    if (idle < assets) {
        _removeLiquidityForUsdc(assets - idle);
    }
    super._withdraw(caller, receiver, owner, assets, shares);
}
