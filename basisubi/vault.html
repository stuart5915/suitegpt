Market Classification

The Four Regimes

RANGING

Price bouncing between moving averages. Low volatility.
±3% range (tight)
Why: Calm markets = price stays in a tight band. Tight range captures maximum fees per dollar of liquidity.
ETH at $2,000. Range set to $1,940–$2,060. You earn fees on every trade in this $120 window.

STRONG UPTREND

Price above both MAs, trending up cleanly.
±2% range (very tight)
Why: Strong trends are low-volatility. Ultra-tight range maximizes fee capture during the calm grind up.
ETH trending from $2,000 → $2,100 over a week. Range: $1,960–$2,040. Rebalances upward as price climbs.

HIGH VOLATILITY

Large daily swings. Uncertain direction.
±8% range (wide)
Why: Wild swings mean price could move $200 in a day. Wide range ensures you stay in range and keep earning.
ETH swinging between $1,800–$2,200. Range: $1,840–$2,160. You earn on every swing without going out of range.

STRONG DOWNTREND

Price below both MAs, falling.
±4% range (moderate)
Why: Downtrends are volatile but directional. Moderate range balances fee capture with staying in range.
ETH dropping from $2,000 → $1,800. Range: $1,920–$2,080, rebalances downward as price falls.
Decision Flow
What happens every time the vault checks the market.
1

Read ETH price from Chainlink oracle

On-chain price feed. No API keys, no centralized data.

2

Calculate SMA(7), SMA(30), volatility, momentum

Rolling indicators computed from the last 30 days of on-chain price snapshots.

3

Classify regime

Ranging, Strong Uptrend, Strong Downtrend, or High Volatility.

4

Set range width based on regime

±2% for uptrend, ±3% for ranging, ±4% for downtrend, ±8% for volatile.

5

Check: is price within 80% of current range edge?

If price has drifted near the edge, a rebalance is needed to stay efficient.

Yes — Rebalance

Pull liquidity, swap to correct ratio, open new position centered on current price. Stake in Aerodrome gauge.

No — Do Nothing

Current range is still good. Keep earning fees. Check again later.
