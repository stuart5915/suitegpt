Strategy Engine
The Strategy
Simple rules. No predictions. Fully autonomous.

Step 1: Initial Deposit

Deposit USDC and/or ETH in any ratio. The vault swaps your tokens to match the current position's ratio and adds liquidity. Your capital starts earning fees immediately.

Step 2: In Range

While ETH price stays within the vault's range, your liquidity earns trading fees from every swap. AERO gauge rewards accumulate and get compounded into ETH.

Step 3: Pushed Out

When price moves beyond the range, the vault holds 100% of one token. No forced swaps. Anyone can call rebalance() to reposition — depositors are naturally incentivized since out-of-range capital earns zero fees.

Step 4: Reposition

The vault repositions using ONLY what it holds — no swapping between ETH and USDC.

Pushed above (holding USDC): Provide USDC below current price. Range: −20%.
Pushed below (holding ETH): Provide ETH above current price. Range: +20%.

Repeat forever.
The Rules
1. Start 50/50 ETH/USDC at ±10% range
2. When out of range → reposition single-sided ±20%
3. Holding ETH → provide above current price (sell on recovery)
4. Holding USDC → provide below current price (buy on dip)
5. NEVER swap between ETH and USDC on rebalance
6. AERO rewards → always converted to ETH (accumulation bias)
7. ETH reserve deployed into LP when vault repositions to ETH-side
8. No admin. No owner. No keeper. Fully permissionless.

AERO Compounding

Anyone can call compound() at any time. It claims AERO rewards from the gauge, swaps them to ETH, and holds them in the vault's ETH reserve. This ETH gets deployed into the LP on the next rebalance or deposit. Over time, the vault accumulates more and more ETH — that's the point.

Deposits & Withdrawals

Deposit USDC, ETH, or both in any ratio. The vault swaps to match the current position and adds your capital to the LP immediately. Withdraw anytime — you receive your proportional share of ETH + USDC. No lock-up. No forced swaps on exit.

Why Permissionless Matters

There is no admin, no owner, no pause button. Every function — deposit, withdraw, rebalance, compound — can be called by anyone. Once deployed, the vault runs forever as long as the chain is live. No one can change the rules or access your funds.
