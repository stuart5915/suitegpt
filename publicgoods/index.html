Why Public Goods?

The best infrastructure is the kind everyone can use. We build tools that are free to access, open by default, and funded by the value they create — not by locking users in.

Every project here is either free to use, open-source, or returns value directly to its community. No VC gates. No paywalls on education. No extraction.

Community-Owned
